# Listing-Texte (SpigotMC / Modrinth) — Entwurf

**Titel:** ReTag — Nametags, Prefixes & Tab Sorting [NametagEdit successor]
**Tagline (Modrinth):** Modern nametag prefixes & suffixes with tab sorting. Imports your NametagEdit data automatically.

**Beschreibung (Kern):**
NametagEdit hasn't been updated since 2023. ReTag is a lightweight, modern replacement built for Paper 1.21+:
drop it in, and your existing NametagEdit groups and player tags are imported automatically on first start —
your /nte commands keep working too.
✔ Group & per-player prefixes/suffixes  ✔ Tab-list sorting by group weight
✔ Legacy & codes AND MiniMessage gradients  ✔ No dependencies, tiny jar  ✔ Actively maintained for every new Minecraft version — that's the whole point of this plugin.
